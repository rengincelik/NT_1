For 15 years I walked around like a zombie and blamed it on "engineering stress". When my battery died out of nowhere in the middle of the day, or my knees buckled when I laughed, I called it "just being busy".

Turns out it has a name: Narcolepsy Type 1.

Everyone around us says "we're all tired, don't make a fuss", and doctors just say "you're not sleeping enough", so it never occurs to you. Noticing a fault in your own body can take years. In Turkey, thousands of people are still groping around in the dark and running into walls.

If you have ever had those moments where you quietly think "something is off with me, nobody can be this exhausted", you don't need to read long articles. There is a practical 5-question screening test used in clinics, the Swiss Narcolepsy Scale (SNS). It took me 15 years to learn this. I put it here so you can check in 2 minutes.
